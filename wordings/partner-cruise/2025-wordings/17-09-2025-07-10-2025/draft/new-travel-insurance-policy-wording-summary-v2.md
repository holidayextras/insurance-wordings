# New Travel Insurance Policy Wording Summary v2

New Travel Insurance Policy Wording

Background

As part of Consumer Duty, we have reviewed our policy wordings across our business to improve readability for customers. We have recently finalised the travel wording and will be rolling this out across our client base in the coming months.

Aim

The aim of the review was to improve readability by using shorter words and sentences as well as reordering the document.

It was not the intention to amend cover except where greater clarity was required.

Summary of Changes

Hyperlinks

We have introduced hyperlinks into the wording that allows customers to move around the document more easily.

Ticks and crosses

Throughout the wording we have introduced ticks, crosses and other punctuation marks, to indicate positive and negatives for example, ticks for what is covered and crosses to highlight exclusions.

Use of colour

The plan is to make greater use of colour for example, shading the “what is covered” sections green and exclusions in red. The colouring will be dependent on the brand guidelines used by clients.

Reordering the sections

The Virgin Money wording was reordered in 2024 and the new wording goes along similar lines with the Contact Details, How to Make a Claim and Table of Benefits all being brought forward to the front of the wording.

Definitions

The number of definitions has been reduced, with greater reliance on the standard meaning of words.

In addition, in line with good market practice we have also included definitions in the cover sections that are specific to that section. This has meant moving a number of definitions into the cover sections.

Cover sections

The design of the cover sections has been altered so there is a table of benefits relating to that particular section of cover appearing at the top of the section followed by any relevant definitions.

What is and What is not covered items are shown with ticks and crosses. Any conditions applying to the section are highlighted with an exclamation mark.

Other important information

A number of sections that previously appeared in the front part of the wording have been moved to the back. These are generally sections that can be considered less important to the customer such as how to renew and the insurer details.

Appendices

In previous iterations of the policy a list of countries has been included to show what countries are covered by each geographical area we rate against for example, Worldwide including USA, Canada, etc. This has been moved to the end of the policy.

Similarly, the hazardous activities table has been moved into the Appendices section. The opportunity has been taken to standardise the activities where possible across the schemes. There are some schemes such as JLU’s BIBA schemes that have bespoke activity lists which will remain.

Changes in cover

The aim was not to change the cover provided by the insurance however, to improve clarity of the cover intentions some changes have been made. Some of these follow FOS decisions where they have decided that cover is provided under the policy.

Emergency medical expenses

This section has historically had an exclusion for all UK only trips. This was a blunt tool to reinforce the fact that medical costs would be picked up by the NHS. However, in doing so

It meant other costs such as repatriating someone to their home in Scotland following hospitalisation in Cornwall would technically be excluded.

Whilst some policies included a nominal limit for UK trips, for example Holiday Extras Direct has a £2,000 limit. The policy has been reworded so it makes clear that non-medical and funeral expenses costs are not covered for UK trip, but other elements are such as repatriation.

Baggage delay, missed departure and travel delay

In addition to the expansion of cover for UK trips under Emergency Medical Expenses, we have removed the restriction on these sections that excluded UK trips.

Missed connections

Some of our schemes include missed connection cover but the wording in the Missed Departure section has been updated to make this clearer. This cover will now apply across all schemes going forward.

Event tickets

Event tickets were previously covered under the Passport and Other Documents section; these have now been moved into the Personal Money section.
