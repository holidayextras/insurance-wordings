### Criteria for Purchase
Confirm that you have read, understood, agree with and
meet the above Eligibility Conditions and Statements.
To be eligible for cover under this Policy, you confirm and
agree that:
You are a resident and must have an address in the United
Kingdom, the Channel Islands or the Isle of Man and lived
here for at least 6 months in the last 12 months.
The purchaser of this policy must be 18 years of age or older
at the time of purchase. If the insured person is aged under
18 then this policy can only be purchased by a parent or
guardian of that person. Where this is not the case please call
our call centre.

Medical conditions existing before
you bought your policy
Is anyone travelling:
- currently having or awaiting surgery, a procedure, hospital
treatment, dialysis or cancer treatment?
- awaiting tests, test results or investigations for a diagnosed
condition?
- been diagnosed with a terminal illness?
- been treated for alcohol or drug addiction in the last five
years?
Is anyone travelling experiencing any undiagnosed
symptoms?
Has any traveller ever had:
- a heart attack, angina, or any other heart condition?
- high blood pressure, blood clots, raised cholesterol,
aneurysm, or any circulatory disease?
- a stroke, mini-stroke (TIA), or brain haemorrhage?
- a lung or respiratory-related condition (not including
stable, well-controlled asthma)?
- any bowel/abdominal conditions e.g. gallstones,
diverticulitis, IBS and Crohns disease?
- any form of cancer, whether in remission or not?
In the last 2 years has any traveller had any physical or
psychological conditions requiring treatment, consultations,
investigations, or medication?
If there are any changes in your health before you travel,
such as new or increased medication, any referrals for tests
or a specialist appointment, or a new diagnosis/course of
treatment, you must tell us, failure to do so could invalidate
ANY future claims.
